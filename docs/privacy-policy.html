VyparHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

VyparHub helps retail shops browse FMCG products, place orders, receive order updates, and manage delivery information. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, shop name, role, and login data.
  • Shop and delivery details: address, area, city, state, and pincode.
  • Order data: products ordered, quantities, totals, order status, and order notes.
  • Payment metadata: payment mode, amount, payment status, and payment provider reference.
  • Device messaging data: Firebase Cloud Messaging token for order and promotional notifications.
  • App diagnostics and analytics: crash reports and analytics events through Firebase services.

Information We Do Not Collect

VyparHub does not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV, or net banking passwords. Online payments are handled by Razorpay.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To show products, manage cart, checkout, delivery, and order history.
  • To send OTPs, order updates, and promotional notifications.
  • To process payments and reconcile payment status.
  • To improve reliability, diagnose crashes, and understand app usage.

Third-Party Services

VyparHub may share limited data with these providers only as needed:

  • Firebase for analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications.
  • Razorpay for payment processing.
  • SMS/OTP providers such as MSG91 for phone verification.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers used to operate the service.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS in production. Passwords are hashed before storage. Access to admin APIs is protected with authentication and role checks.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account, address, order, and payment metadata as long as needed for service, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal requirements. To request account deletion or data correction, contact VyparHub support.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact: support@vyparhub.example
