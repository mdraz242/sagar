Account Deletion

Last updated: 8 August 2026

VyparHub lets you delete your account and associated data at any time. You can do this two ways:

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the VyparHub app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Verify your identity via OTP when prompted.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account is scheduled for deletion immediately after confirmation.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

Email support@vyparhub.com from the email or phone number registered on your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We verify the request and delete the account within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Profile information: name, phone number, email, shop name, saved addresses.
  • Cart, wishlist, wallet balance, loyalty points, and saved payment methods.
  • Notification tokens and device data linked to your account.

What we retain, and why

  • Completed order history, invoices, and tax records — retained as required under Indian tax and business record-keeping law.
  • Fraud-prevention and abuse logs — retained for a limited period to protect other users and the platform.
  • Records required to resolve an open order, return, refund, or dispute at the time of your request — retained until that record is closed, then deleted per the above.

Retained records are kept only as long as legally required and are not used for marketing or any purpose beyond the stated compliance/legal need.

Timeline

In-app deletion requests are processed immediately. Email requests are verified and completed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation once deletion is complete.

Contact

Questions about account deletion or data retention: support@vyparhub.com.
