Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

VyparHub is a B2B FMCG ordering app for kirana and retail stores. We collect only the information needed to create accounts, verify stores, deliver orders, support customers, and improve reliability.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, phone number, optional email, shop name, and password where password login is used.
  • Delivery details such as shop address, city, pincode, selected delivery zone, and location permission data when you choose current location.
  • Order, cart, return, refund, wallet, reward, coupon, and referral activity.
  • Device and diagnostics information through Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics.

How We Use Data

We use this data to verify OTP login through MSG91, deliver orders, show serviceable zones and fees, manage returns and refunds, send order updates and promotional notifications, prevent abuse, and improve app stability.

Third Parties

We use MSG91 for OTP delivery, Firebase for analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications, Supabase/PostgreSQL for database hosting, Render for API hosting, and payment providers such as Razorpay when online payment is activated.

Data Deletion

You can request account deletion from the app profile section or contact support. Certain records such as invoices, tax records, fraud-prevention logs, and completed order history may be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

Contact

Email support@vyparhub.com for privacy or deletion requests.
