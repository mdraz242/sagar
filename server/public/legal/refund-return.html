Refund / Return Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Cancellation Window

Customers may cancel orders while the order status is placed, confirmed, or packed. Orders cannot normally be cancelled after shipping begins.

Return Window

Delivered orders may be eligible for return or exchange within 7 days of delivery, depending on item condition, product type, and business policy.

Eligible Reasons

  • Wrong item delivered.
  • Damaged or defective item.
  • Missing quantity or pack mismatch.
  • Expired or unusable product.

Refund Method

COD refunds may be routed to VyparHub wallet, store credit, or another agreed method. Prepaid refunds may be routed to the original payment method when payment gateway support is active.

Admin Review

All return, exchange, and refund requests may be reviewed by the VyparHub team before approval. Approved wallet refunds are reflected in the customer wallet ledger.
