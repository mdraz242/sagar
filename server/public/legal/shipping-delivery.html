Shipping / Delivery Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Serviceable Areas

VyparHub delivers only to configured serviceable delivery zones. The app checks your selected address pincode against the live delivery zones managed by the admin team.

Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time is shown in the app based on your matched delivery zone. If your pincode is not serviceable, the app will show that delivery is not currently available in your area.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fee and free-delivery threshold are calculated from the delivery zone configured by VyparHub. Fees may vary by location, order value, stock availability, and operational constraints.

Receiving Orders

Please keep your phone reachable and ensure someone at the shop can receive and verify the order. Failed delivery attempts may delay or cancel the order.
